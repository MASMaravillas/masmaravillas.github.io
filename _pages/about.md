I am a doctoral student specializing in Cellular and Molecular Biology. I am interested in the advanced applications of statistics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence in omics sciences and in the use of bioinformatics tools and computational methods in elucidating disease etiologies and contributing to the development of therapeutics. Currently, I am a researcher of the Piontkivska Lab where we are looking into the roles and impacts of post-transcriptional modifications on the progression of diseases and their potential vulnerabilities. 
<br>
<br> 
### Researcher Interests
* High-throughput sequencing data analysis
* Computational biology (e.g., transcriptomics, network analysis)
* Causal inference pipelines
* Statistical modeling and biostatistics
